FORENSIC SYSTEM METHODOLOGY /// TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
01. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & MULTI-SIGNAL PROTOCOL

DeepShield is designed as a digital media evidence inspection workstation. Rather than relying on single-model predictions, DeepShield fuses three independent evidence signals to identify visual, frequency, and behavioral manipulation signatures:

  • VISUAL SPATIAL CNN (EfficientNetB0): Evaluates facial crop boundary blending, pixel-level artifacts, and feature spatial geometry.
  • 2D MAGNITUDE FREQUENCY SPECTRUM (FFT): Computes 2D Fast Fourier Transform to isolate high-frequency energy ratio anomalies characteristic of generative neural models.
  • TEMPORAL EYE BLINKING (MediaPipe EAR): Calculates Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) across frame sequences to flag unnatural blink rates and static eye closures.

03. SYSTEM LIMITATIONS & FORENSIC DISCLAIMERS

• Output represents an AI-assisted detection confidence score based on feature heuristics and visual CNN models.

• High media compression or low resolution may impact high-frequency spectral ratios.

• Primary face crop scope evaluates the dominant detected facial region per frame.
